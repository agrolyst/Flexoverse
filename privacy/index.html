YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flexoverse collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit flexoverse.online or use Flexoverse features. It applies to account registration, Google Sign-In, profiles, posts, photos, reels, comments, likes, friend connections, notifications, and private chats.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: your email address, account identifier, username, display name, and authentication/session information. If you register with an email and password, the authentication provider processes the credentials needed to sign you in; Flexoverse does not need to display or store your password in your public profile.
  • Google Sign-In information: when you choose Continue with Google, Google provides the basic account information permitted by the sign-in flow, such as your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile photo (where available). We use this information to authenticate you, create or connect your Flexoverse account, populate your initial profile, and help you recognise your account.
  • Profile information: username, full name, biography, avatar/profile photo, profile visibility choice, verification or membership status where applicable, and other information you choose to add.
  • Private profile details: an optional address or general location that you enter in profile setup. This is stored separately from public profile fields and is intended to be accessible only to your signed-in account.
  • User content: posts, captions, photos, reels/videos, comments, likes, and other material you upload or create.
  • Messages and conversations: message text, voice-message files, conversation membership, message timestamps, replies, reactions, and related chat information needed to provide messaging.
  • Social and notification activity: friend requests and accepted connections, likes, comments, mentions or message-related notification events where supported, notification read status, blocks, and timestamps.
  • Device and usage information: basic technical information that your browser, device, hosting, and authentication services may process to deliver pages, maintain sign-in sessions, diagnose errors, protect the service, and keep it reliable. Flexoverse also stores selected preferences, such as theme, text size, reduced-motion choice, and chat favourites, in your browser's local storage.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; authenticate users; display and maintain profiles; publish and deliver posts, photos, and reels; provide friend connections, chats, voice messages, and notifications; remember user preferences; respond to support requests; maintain security; prevent misuse; troubleshoot the service; and meet legal obligations.

3. Google user data and Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is an optional way to create an account or sign in. Flexoverse requests basic identity information needed for authentication, principally the Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information such as name and profile photo when available. We use this information only to authenticate you, create or connect your Flexoverse account, populate your profile, and operate account-related features.

Flexoverse does not use Google user data to serve advertising, sell it, or build advertising profiles. We do not use it for purposes unrelated to providing and maintaining the Flexoverse account and features you request. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other Google content as part of the basic sign-in flow.

Google user data is stored and processed by our authentication and hosting provider, Supabase, as needed to operate account authentication and the Flexoverse service. We do not disclose Google user data to third parties except to service providers acting on our behalf, when you direct us to share information through a Flexoverse feature, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety. We do not allow service providers to use Google user data for their own advertising purposes.

Our handling of information received from Google is limited to the practices described in this policy and is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke Flexoverse's access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access may prevent Google Sign-In from working until you reconnect or use another available sign-in method.

4. How information is stored and protected

Flexoverse uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and file storage. Information is transmitted between your browser and the service over HTTPS. Access to account data is intended to be limited by account authentication and database access controls. Reels and chat media are stored in managed storage and may be delivered through temporary signed links. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password and avoid sharing sensitive information in posts or chats.

5. When information is shared

  • With other users: public profile fields and content you publish as public may be viewed by anyone. Information shared in a direct conversation is available to the conversation participants. People who can view content may copy, screenshot, or share it.
  • With service providers: Supabase processes authentication, database, and storage information to host and operate Flexoverse. Google processes sign-in requests when you choose Google Sign-In. These providers process information under their service terms and privacy documentation.
  • For legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed where required by law, valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, our rights, or the security of the service.
  • Business changes: if the service is reorganised, transferred, or acquired, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable privacy requirements.

We do not sell personal information. We do not make private chat content public as part of normal operation.

6. Public and private information

Content you mark public, public profile information, and public reels are intended to be visible beyond your accepted friends. A private profile setting does not necessarily make a separately published public post private. Private address information is intended to remain restricted to your own signed-in account. Do not post information you would not want other people to see.

7. Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

Flexoverse and its authentication provider may use browser storage or similar technologies to maintain your sign-in session and remember settings. Local storage is also used for interface preferences such as theme, text size, reduced motion, and chat favourites. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings; doing so may sign you out or reset preferences. Your browser may separately process technical cookies or storage required by the hosting and authentication services.

8. Retention and account deletion

We retain account information, content, and activity while needed to provide the service, maintain account security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Some information may remain in protected backups for a limited period under the storage provider's backup processes. The current app may not provide a self-service account deletion control. To request account closure or deletion of personal information, email support@agrolystfarms.com from your account email and include your Flexoverse username. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some information may be retained where the law permits or requires it.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can review and update profile information in Settings, choose public or private profile visibility, manage blocks, control what you publish, and adjust available notification display preferences. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the address below. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 where applicable.

10. Children's privacy

Flexoverse is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

11. International processing

Flexoverse and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take steps intended to provide appropriate protection for international transfers in accordance with applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features, providers, or legal obligations change. We will publish the latest version on this page and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how information is used, we will take additional steps to notify users where required by law.
